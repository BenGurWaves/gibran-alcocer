Pianist · Composer · Producer

Born and working in Mérida, Yucatán. Author of the Idea series — solo piano, recorded at home, released only when finished. Streamed by tens of millions; performed, for the first time abroad, in the Grand Hall of the Elbphilharmonie, Hamburg, last June.

My intention is to invite reflection — to take a pause, to remember those quiet moments that sometimes feel so far away.

G.A., on the writing of Idea 12
